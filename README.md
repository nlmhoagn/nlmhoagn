## Profile

I am **Nguyen Le Minh Hoang**, a Computer Engineering undergraduate at **Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT - VNU-HCM)** (GPA: 4.00/4.00). I am an active research member at **ACLab** (Advanced Computing Lab) and **URA Lab** (Understanding and Reasoning with AI Group).

My work bridges the gap between hardware and intelligence: designing real-time vision pipelines, deploying optimized neural models on low-power edge accelerators, and building high-performance multimodal search architectures.
